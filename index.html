How a pilot works

Start with the problem, not the platform.

A focused pilot begins with one live corridor, planning question or recurring operational constraint. The work stays concrete from day one.

Frame the decision

Agree the operational question, the people who own it and what a useful answer must contain.

Connect the evidence

Bring together the relevant movement data, rules and working assumptions. Anonymised samples are fine.

Test the options

Compare interventions against capacity, service and operational constraints using a shared view.

Leave with something useful

Receive a documented analysis, recommended actions and a practical next-step roadmap your team can keep.
