Data & map notes

Train locations are TD berth estimates, not GPS. Hollow dots use approximate route geometry; solid dots use checked mapped track. Exact line and berth are not established. Unverified locations stay in the service list but are not plotted. Satellite imagery is a historical background. Following uses incoming positions and does not predict movement.

Reported delays without a timestamp remain visible as “Age unknown”. Reports with timestamps older than 15 minutes are excluded. After 60 seconds without a snapshot, observations are hidden. Missing delay data never means on time.

Clusters group at least three delayed services within 3 km of a seed. They highlight nearby delays, not confirmed queues or capacity bottlenecks. Counts may include reports whose age is unknown. Headcodes and approximate berth positions can be ambiguous.
