# Rail Insights identity

## Brand idea

**Find the decisive route.**

The symbol combines an `RI` monogram with a railway junction. Several paths enter a shared decision point; one signal-coloured route leaves with clarity. It represents Rail Insights turning complex network evidence into a confident operational decision.

The mark is deliberately not a train silhouette. This gives the identity room to grow beyond a single product into analytics, research, planning, operations, and infrastructure services.

## Colour system

| Role | Colour | Hex |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Network ink | Near black | `#10130f` |
| Signal route | Acid yellow-green | `#d9ff43` |
| Operational paper | Warm off-white | `#f0efe8` |
| Incident accent | Orange | `#ef5b2a` |
| Digital blue | Cobalt | `#1857c9` |

Use the signal colour sparingly. It should identify the chosen route, current state, or primary action—not become a general background colour.

## Typography

- **Manrope** for headlines, body copy, and product language
- **DM Mono** for timings, labels, train describers, system states, and small technical details

The contrast between human-readable grotesk type and operational monospace is part of the identity.

## Usage rules

- Keep clear space around the lockup equal to the diameter of its central junction.
- Use the full lockup when the brand is being introduced; use the mark only when context already identifies Rail Insights.
- Do not rotate, outline, shadow, bevel, stretch, recolour, or place the logo over visually busy imagery.
- Do not replace the signal route colour with multiple decorative colours.
- At very small sizes, use the standalone mark rather than the full wordmark.

## Voice

Clear, operational, and evidence-led. Prefer direct language such as “See the network. Decide sooner.” Avoid exaggerated technology claims, generic innovation language, and unexplained AI terminology.

## Before formal registration

The concept is designed to be visually distinctive, but a professional trademark and similarity search should be completed before registration or a large-scale commercial rollout.
